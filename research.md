    <p class="theme-number">01</p>
    <h2><span class="heading-highlight cyan">Systems Immunology &amp; Vaccinology</span></h2>
    <p>I combine longitudinal multi-omics data with computational modeling to understand why people respond differently to infection and vaccination. Recent work focuses on pertussis vaccine responses, immune polarization, B-cell receptor repertoires, and reproducible community challenges that put predictive models to a rigorous test.</p>

<section class="research-theme" id="network-biology">

    <p class="theme-number">02</p>
    <h2><span class="heading-highlight orange">Network Biology &amp; Mitochondrial Genomics</span></h2>
    <p>Genes and mutations rarely act alone. This body of work uses co-occurrence networks, spectral methods, multilayer models, and higher-order interactions to reveal structure in mitochondrial genomes and complex biological systems—from adaptation across human populations to cancer and organismal development.</p>
